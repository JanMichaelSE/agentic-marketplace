# Wait What

Wait — I do not understand where you have got to here. Re-pitch that: give me
a little context, use ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, and use the
ubiquitous language from the project's established `GLOSSARY.md` or
`CONTEXT.md`. Follow `GLOSSARY-MAP.md` or `CONTEXT-MAP.md` to the relevant

ask which glossary applies. If no glossary exists, use the terminology already
